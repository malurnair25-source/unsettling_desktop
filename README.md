# Mary Jane — An Interactive Mystery

## The Story

Mary Jane was an ordinary young woman living alone. To anyone looking from the outside, her life seemed relatively normal: she had a few daily conversations, used social media and search engines, took pictures with her digital camera, and kept a personal diary.

But on April 18, 2024, Mary Jane disappeared.

The last few weeks before her disappearance contain a series of strange details that don't seem connected at first.

Her browser history looks mostly ordinary, with searches about medication mixed in with one particularly suspicious search involving sulfuric acid and its availability in unusually large quantities.

Her messages reveal that she had been communicating regularly with a mysterious man. Although he initially appears to be a stranger, their conversations suggest that they knew each other well. Their messages become increasingly concerning, especially when they refer to something that happened the previous night and a secret that "no soul" was supposed to discover.

Mary Jane also appears to have been planning to meet this man again.

Among her files is a password-protected personal diary containing entries about her schizophrenia episodes. One entry describes seeing a man in a tailcoat standing outside her apartment window and smiling at her.

At first, it is tempting to dismiss the strange events as part of her episodes.

But her digital camera tells a different story.

Mary Jane had sent the mysterious man her camera as a birthday gift because she couldn't afford anything else. The camera was connected to a folder on her computer, meaning that photographs deleted from the camera could still potentially be recovered.

Inside the Recycle Bin are photographs that Mary Jane had deleted.

Some of the recovered photographs appear to show a deteriorating building covered in strange symbols. The photographs are dated April 18 — the same day Mary Jane disappeared.

Then there is one more photograph.

It was deleted on April 8.

The photograph appears to show Mary Jane inside her apartment, working on her laptop, with her window visible behind her.

The further you investigate her computer, the more the timeline begins to come together.

Her messages.
Her diary.
Her photographs.
Her deleted files.
The mysterious man.
The building.
And finally, the events of April 18.

Your task is to explore Mary Jane's computer and piece together what happened before she disappeared.


## Objective

Explore the computer, examine the files, recover deleted photographs, read the messages and diary entries, and reconstruct the events leading up to Mary Jane's disappearance.

The answer is hidden somewhere in the computer.

You just have to find it.
